# Teams, roles and security

## Workspaces
Links, QR codes, documents and analytics belong to a **workspace**, not to a person. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the menu at the top left.

## Roles
| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, transferring ownership and deleting the workspace |
| Admin | Invite, suspend and remove members, rename the workspace, view the audit log |
| Member | Create, edit and delete links, QR codes and documents |
| Viewer | View everything and download QR codes and reports |

Only the owner can grant or manage the Admin role.

## Invitations
Invitations are single-use links that expire after 7 days and only work for the invited email address. Pending invitations can be revoked at any time.

## Suspending and removing
**Suspend** a member to block their access instantly while keeping their history. **Remove** takes them out of the workspace without affecting their account or other workspaces.

## Audit log
Admins can see who changed what, when and from where, and export it as CSV.

## Your account
In **Profile → Security** you can change your password (which signs out your other devices), review active sessions, and sign out devices you don't recognize.
