# Short links

## Custom back-halves
Choose a memorable ending such as `lynqr.app/s/summer-sale`: 3–64 letters, numbers, dashes or underscores. You can rename it later, but the old address stops working. Printed QR codes are unaffected because they use their own scan address.

## Change the destination any time
Edit a link's destination and every place it's shared, including QR codes that use it, follows instantly.

## Password protection
Visitors must enter the password before they're redirected. Lynqr never reveals the destination on the password page and remembers a correct password on that device for 12 hours. Change the password to sign everyone out.

## UTM campaign tags
Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term` and `utm_content` once, and Lynqr appends them to the destination on every visit, so Google Analytics and similar tools attribute the traffic. Tags already on your destination URL are never overwritten. Use the presets (Newsletter, Instagram, Print/QR…) to stay consistent.

## Pause, expiration and fallback
- **Pause** a link to stop redirects temporarily.
- **Expire** it automatically at a date and time.
- Set a **fallback URL** to send visitors somewhere useful instead of the "expired" page.

Paused and expired visits are not counted as clicks.
