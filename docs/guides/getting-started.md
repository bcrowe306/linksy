# Getting started with Lynqr

Lynqr turns any destination into a **short link** and a **QR code** you can change later and measure as people use it.

## 1. Create a short link

1. Click **Create → Short link** (or press <kbd>N</kbd> then <kbd>L</kbd>).
2. Paste the destination, for example `https://example.com/spring-menu`.
3. Optionally choose a custom back-half such as `spring-menu`, so the link reads `lynqr.app/s/spring-menu`.
4. Click **Create link**. It works immediately.

## 2. Turn it into a QR code

On the **Short Links** page, click the QR icon on any link. Lynqr creates a dynamic QR code for it and opens **QR Studio**, where you can choose a template, colors, a logo and a "Scan me" frame. The preview shows exactly what you'll download, and the badge tells you if the design still scans reliably.

## 3. Share and measure

Every click and scan appears on the **Dashboard** in real time, with country, city, device and traffic source. Open **Analytics** for date ranges, maps, heatmaps and exports.

## 4. Invite your team

Go to **Team & Settings → Members → Invite**. Choose a role (Admin, Member or Viewer) and share the invite link.

> **Tip:** Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere to see every keyboard shortcut.
