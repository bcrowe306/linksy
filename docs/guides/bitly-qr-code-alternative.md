# Lynqr vs. Bitly & Uniqode: transparent QR codes and short links

Most teams looking for a dynamic QR code and short-link platform run into the same three headaches:

1. **Bait-and-switch "free" generators** that deactivate printed QR codes after a 14-day trial.
2. **Per-user and tier-locked pricing** where adding team members, exporting SVG vector files or replacing a PDF menu requires jumping from a starter plan to a $35–$100+/month tier.
3. **Split tools** where short links live in one app, QR codes in another, and hosted PDF menus on a separate file host.

Lynqr was built to replace all three with a single, transparent workspace.

## Key reasons teams switch to Lynqr {#why-teams-switch}

### 1. Flat workspace pricing with unlimited seats {#flat-pricing}
Whether you are a solo founder, a restaurant group with five managers, or an agency collaborating with clients, Lynqr costs one flat rate per workspace. Invite your entire team with granular role-based permissions (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Member`, `Viewer`) and a full audit log at no extra cost.

### 2. Replace PDF menus and brochures without reprinting {#hosted-pdfs}
When you upload a PDF to Lynqr, we host it on fast global storage behind a dynamic QR code. When seasonal prices change or you fix a typo in a brochure, upload the new PDF to the same document entry—every printed table tent, sticker and flyer immediately serves the updated file.

### 3. Live scannability verification in QR Studio {#qr-studio}
Adding a center logo, brand gradient or custom eye shapes shouldn't be guesswork. Lynqr's QR Studio verifies contrast and test-decodes your QR code in real time as you design it, warning you before you download if a color combination might fail on older phone cameras.

### 4. Built-in short links with UTM tags and password protection {#short-links}
Every dynamic QR code is backed by a clean short link, and you can create standalone branded short links with custom slugs, automatic UTM campaign parameters, expiration dates, fallback URLs and password protection.
