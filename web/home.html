Questions, answered

What is a dynamic QR code?+

A dynamic QR code points to a short Lynqr address that forwards to your destination. Because the redirect lives on Lynqr, you can change the destination at any time without reprinting the code, and every scan is counted.

Do Lynqr's free static QR codes ever expire?+

Never. Codes created with our free QR code generator encode your URL, Wi-Fi network, or contact card directly inside the QR pattern. There is no account required, no scan limit, and no 14-day trial trap.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?+

No. Every workspace gets 7 days of Lynqr Pro without a card. Subscribe whenever you're ready; any trial days you have left carry over.

What happens when my trial ends?+

Your workspace becomes read-only and its links and QR codes pause until you subscribe. Nothing is deleted, and everything starts working again the moment you subscribe.

Is the price per user?+

No. Lynqr Pro is one flat price per workspace with unlimited members, links and QR codes.

Can I cancel any time?+

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription yourself from the Billing page. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
